# Monthly Routine

- [ ] 1. Pay the rent (68,000 yen, from Rakuten Bank)
- [ ] 2. File the overtime request (put it on the calendar; connect to the VPN first when working remotely)
- [ ] 3. Set aside this month's savings (plan: 170,000 yen a month to clear the scholarship)
  - 170,000 yen a month saves 1.5 million yen and repays the scholarship around next March; even 100,000 yen a month repays it in full by next September
- [ ] 4. Scholarship prepayment
  - Applied by around the 13th (9 business days before the debit day): debited on the 27th of this month (the next business day if the 27th is a bank holiday)
  - Applied from around the 14th to the end of the month: debited on the 27th of next month
  - For back-to-back prepayments, the next application opens from around the 5th to the 13th of the month after the debit
  - [ ] Apply on Scholarnet PS: https://scholar-ps.sas.jasso.go.jp/mypage/todokede_open.do
  - [ ] Check the processing status on Scholarnet PS
