# Uncomplete Tasks

Use the Todoist connector to fetch incomplete tasks (find-tasks, filter "all", limit 100) and report briefly in English (keep task titles as they are written in Todoist).

- Exclude Todoist tutorial/onboarding tasks (e.g. "Turn any email into a task", "Capture: add your first task").
- Group them in this order: overdue, P1, due soon (within the next 2 weeks); for the rest, give only the count.
- Show subtasks grouped under their parent task.
- Read-only: do not complete or modify any tasks.
